Monday & Friday Route

    
THE RUN.

    
One 2.5-hour loop covers all return pickups, all grocery shopping, all return drop-offs, and all grocery deliveries. This is the full batch route both days.

    

      

        
2.5 hrs

        
total route time

      

      

        
Mon+Fri

        
pickup days

      

    

    

      
Full Monday / Friday Schedule

      
9:00 am
Order cutoff — confirm all grocery orders, collect payment before leaving

      
10:00 am
Start route — Campus North (stop 1) · collect R&N box

      
10:12
Max Palevsky (stop 2) · collect box, drop any grocery orders

      
10:22
Breckinridge (stop 3) · collect box

      
10:30
Burton-Judson (stop 4) · collect box

      
10:38
Renee GG (stop 5) · collect box

      
10:46
Woodlawn (stop 6) · collect box · south campus done

      
11:00 am
Trader Joe's (1528 E 55th) — shop ALL grocery orders at once (35–40 min)

      
11:45
UPS Store (53rd St) — drop ALL returns, scan QR codes, photograph receipts

      
12:05 pm
Deliver groceries to each dorm on the way back home · text each customer "5 min away"

      
12:30 pm
Done. Send receipt photos to all return customers via DM.

    

    

      
Why this route is efficient

      
You're already going to TJ's and UPS. Picking up packages from dorms on the way costs near-zero extra time — you're walking through campus anyway. Batching all grocery orders means one big shop covers 5–8 customers at once. Total work time: ~5 hours/week for both days. That's it.
